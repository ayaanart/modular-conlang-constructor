### **§5. Precedence**
(1) If a **Non-Master Manual** the **Master Manual**,
or states something not stated in the **Master Manual**,
then the other manual is wrong and the **Master Manual** is correct.

(2) If the **Master Manual** contradicts itself,
then it becomes a **Non-Master Manual**,
and then the previous **Master Manual** that was in use before the manual with the error was introduced will be in use again;
it will dictate the entire workings of the **MCC**.
> Note: A **Master Manual** contradicting itself is a significant error and should be reported immediately.
